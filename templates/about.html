About Gaming Universe

Who we are

Gaming Universe is an educational and curatorial site focused on multiplayer and competitive gaming. Our goal is to provide clear, well-researched writeups about the mechanics, community, and competitive footprint of major titles — helping players, students and content creators quickly understand what matters about each game.

Research & editorial methodology

We aggregate developer announcements, industry reporting, player statistics (Steam charts, MAU lists), and esports trackers to build an informed summary for each title. For competitive analysis we look at: average monthly players, viewership numbers, tournament structure, and community ecosystem.

Use for your project

Feel free to reuse content for educational projects. Cite sources where necessary; if you want a custom export (PDF or slide) for your college submission, I can prepare print-friendly pages.
